Which designs let PULSE personalise

One row per simulated design, ranked by how much of its calls PULSE could answer from the narrowest group: personalized first, then tailored_B, then tailored_A, then population. Next to each share is the day of the intervention that distribution was first chosen. Click any column to re-rank.

Ranking

Both tailored columns are a demographic cohort, the fifty nearest by age after matching on sex. tailored_B is the same-intervention cohort: at least 10 of its interactions in the window are needed. tailored_A is the across-intervention cohort, everyone sharing the same disease: at least 10 of its interactions in the window are needed. A simulation with a single intervention gives both the same pool, so tailored_B wins whenever either qualifies and tailored_A only appears once other interventions share the disease.

Runs that lost calls to server errors are left out, as are the hundred-participant runs.
