Next to each share is the day of the intervention that distribution was first chosen. Click any column to re-rank.

Ranking

The intervention_specific column is the group carrying the same disease as the participant. No run recorded a disease, so what it measures here is the “not recorded” group — cohort size, not disease. Read it with that in mind.

Runs that lost calls to server errors are left out, as are the hundred-participant runs.
