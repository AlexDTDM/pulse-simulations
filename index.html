Which designs let PULSE personalise

One row per simulated design, ranked by how much of its calls PULSE could answer from the narrowest group: personalized first, then tailored_B, then tailored_A, then population. Next to each share is the day of the intervention that distribution was first chosen. Click any column to re-rank.

Ranking

Both tailored columns are a demographic cohort, the fifty nearest by age after matching on sex. tailored_B is the same-intervention cohort: at least 10 of its interactions in the window are needed. tailored_A is the across-intervention cohort, every participant whatever their intervention: at least 10 of its interactions in the window are needed. Its pool includes every other intervention in the same database, so its neighbours are not all simulated.

Runs that lost calls to server errors are left out, as are the hundred-participant runs.
